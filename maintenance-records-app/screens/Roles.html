Roles

What each role can do. Folder access is set per person and limits this.

Technician

Scan, upload, and edit in folders they're given. Sign task cards when given Sign.

Certifying Engineer

Everything a Technician can do, plus sign releases (CRS).

Planning and Stores

View and download records. Upload parts certificates and planning documents.

Auditor

View everything they're given, see revision history and the activity log. Can't change records.

Folder owner

Manage who can use their folders and approve access requests.

Admin

Add and deactivate people, and manage all folders and roles.
